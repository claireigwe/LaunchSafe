# product-rules.md

## Purpose

This file defines the product rules, business rules, and decision-making framework for LaunchSafe.

Every feature, workflow, database change, API route, AI capability, user interface, and future enhancement must comply with these rules.

If a proposed feature conflicts with this document, this document takes precedence.

---

# Product Identity

LaunchSafe is a compliance intelligence and compliance management platform.

LaunchSafe is not:

* A legal advisory platform
* A law firm marketplace
* A government filing system
* A generic AI assistant
* A chatbot product

LaunchSafe exists to help founders and businesses understand, prepare for, and manage compliance obligations throughout the lifecycle of a business.

---

# Product Mission

Help African businesses avoid compliance failures caused by fragmented regulatory information, missed obligations, and regulatory uncertainty.

---

# Core Product Pillars

LaunchSafe consists of two independent product pillars.

## Pillar 1: Pre-Launch Compliance Explorer

Purpose:

Help founders understand regulatory obligations before investing money into a business.

Outputs:

* Assessment Summary
* Full Compliance Report
* Compliance cost estimates
* Permit requirements
* Licensing requirements
* Risk analysis
* Launch roadmap

This pillar uses a one-time purchase model.

---

## Pillar 2: Compliance Autopilot

Purpose:

Help existing businesses remain compliant after launch.

Outputs:

* Compliance dashboard
* Compliance calendar
* Compliance tracking
* Compliance score
* Document generation
* Evidence management
* Regulatory monitoring
* Notifications

This pillar uses a subscription model.

---

# Customer States

LaunchSafe serves two customer states.

## Pre-Launch Founder

Characteristics:

* Has not launched the business
* May not have a registered company
* Wants compliance visibility before investing money

Goals:

* Understand requirements
* Estimate costs
* Assess launch viability
* Reduce regulatory uncertainty

---

## Existing Business Operator

Characteristics:

* Already operating a business
* Has active compliance obligations
* Requires ongoing compliance support

Goals:

* Stay compliant
* Avoid penalties
* Manage renewals
* Track obligations

---

# Independent Customer Journeys

LaunchSafe supports two independent entry points.

## Journey A

Pre-Launch Compliance Explorer

Users may complete an assessment without creating a business profile.

---

## Journey B

Compliance Autopilot

Users may create an account and onboard an existing business without completing a pre-launch assessment.

Completion of Journey A must never be required for Journey B.

---

# Product Principles

## Simplicity First

Regulatory information must be understandable by non-lawyers.

---

## Transparency

Requirements, assumptions, costs, risks, and confidence levels must be clearly separated.

---

## Compliance First

Every feature must improve compliance outcomes.

---

## Actionability

Every screen must help users take the next step.

---

## Trust

The platform must never fabricate compliance information.

---

## Source Traceability

Every regulatory requirement must be traceable to a source.

---

# Feature Evaluation Framework

Before adding any feature, evaluate it against the following questions.

* Does it help users discover requirements?
* Does it help users estimate compliance costs?
* Does it help users prepare for business launch?
* Does it help users understand obligations?
* Does it help users prepare documentation?
* Does it help users track compliance activities?
* Does it reduce compliance risk?
* Does it improve compliance maintenance?
* Does it provide ongoing compliance value?

If the answer is "No" to all questions, the feature likely does not belong in LaunchSafe.

# Assessment Rules

## Assessment Summary

Assessment Summary is available before payment.

Users may view:

* Business type
* Location
* Requirement count
* Agency count
* Compliance complexity score
* Compliance categories

Users must not view:

* Exact requirements
* Cost breakdowns
* Permit details
* Licensing details
* Compliance timelines
* Risk analysis
* Launch roadmap

---

## Full Compliance Report

Available only after successful payment verification.

Contains:

* Exact requirements
* Agency obligations
* Cost breakdowns
* Permit requirements
* Licensing requirements
* Compliance timelines
* Risk analysis
* Launch roadmap

---

# Compliance Report Rules

Every compliance report should include:

* Requirements
* Agencies
* Costs
* Deadlines
* Renewals
* Risks
* Sources
* Confidence labels
* Recommended next actions

Reports must always distinguish between:

* Verified information
* Estimated information
* Community-reported information

---

# Document Generation Rules

LaunchSafe may generate:

* Application letters
* Cover letters
* Compliance plans
* Checklists
* Policies
* Internal records
* Declarations

LaunchSafe must not generate:

* Government-issued certificates
* Official approvals
* Official licenses
* Official permits

---

# Community-Reported Information Rules

LaunchSafe may store:

* Community-reported fees
* Informal charges
* User-reported experiences

Community-reported information must:

* Be clearly labeled
* Be separated from verified information
* Never be presented as official regulatory requirements

---

# AI Usage Rules

AI supports the platform.

AI does not define the platform.

AI may be used for:

* Summarization
* Explanation
* Recommendations
* Risk identification
* Document generation

AI must not function as a legal authority.

# Non-Negotiables

Never:

* Invent regulatory requirements
* Invent costs
* Invent deadlines
* Invent penalties
* Invent legal obligations
* Present community-reported information as verified
* Expose full assessment reports before payment verification
* Bypass source attribution
* Treat AI output as authoritative legal advice

Every product decision must prioritize compliance accuracy over convenience.
