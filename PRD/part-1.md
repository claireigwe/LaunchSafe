LaunchSafe Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version 1.0

1. Executive Summary
LaunchSafe is a compliance intelligence platform that helps entrepreneurs and businesses understand, prepare for, and manage regulatory obligations before and after launching a business.
The platform addresses one of the largest hidden challenges facing African businesses: fragmented regulatory information and poor compliance visibility.
Many businesses fail not because of weak products or poor management, but because founders encounter unexpected regulatory costs, licensing requirements, inspections, renewals, and penalties after they have already invested significant resources.
LaunchSafe centralizes regulatory intelligence, compliance planning, deadline management, document generation, and risk monitoring into a single platform.
The platform serves businesses during two critical phases:
Pre-Launch
Helping founders understand:
What registrations are required
Which permits are needed
Which agencies are involved
Estimated startup compliance costs
Compliance timelines
Recurring obligations
Post-Launch
Helping businesses:
Track obligations
Generate compliance documents
Manage deadlines
Monitor regulatory changes
Store compliance evidence
Maintain ongoing compliance
LaunchSafe's long-term vision is to become the compliance operating system for African businesses.

2. Problem Statement
Business compliance information is fragmented across multiple agencies and jurisdictions.
A founder launching a business may need information from:
Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)
National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC)
Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)
Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)
State governments
Local government authorities
Industry regulators
Each organization maintains separate processes, terminology, documentation requirements, timelines, and fees.
The result is:
Unexpected costs
Regulatory delays
Compliance violations
Business interruptions
Financial penalties
Failed business launches
The market lacks a centralized platform that converts complex regulatory information into actionable business guidance.

3. Product Vision
To become the operating system for business compliance across Africa.
LaunchSafe should allow any founder to answer five questions instantly:
What do I need?
How much will it cost?
When is it due?
What happens if I miss it?
What should I do next?
without requiring legal expertise.
LaunchSafe should not only explain compliance requirements but also assist businesses in preparing the documentation needed to satisfy those requirements.
LaunchSafe serves businesses both before launch and after launch.
Before launch, users can purchase a one-time compliance assessment and launch roadmap.
Before purchasing the full assessment, users receive an Assessment Summary that provides a high-level view of the compliance landscape for their intended business.
The Assessment Summary may include:
- Business type identified
- Location identified
- Number of requirements discovered
- Number of agencies involved
- Compliance complexity score
- Compliance categories involved

The Full Compliance Report is unlocked only after successful payment verification and includes:
- Exact regulatory requirements
- Agency-specific obligations
- Cost breakdowns
- Compliance timelines
- Permit and licensing requirements
- Risk analysis
- Launch roadmap

After launch, businesses can subscribe to Compliance Autopilot for ongoing compliance management.

5. Product Principles

Transparency

Trust

Actionability

Compliance-First
The platform exists to solve compliance problems, not to showcase AI.

11. Assumptions
Founders prefer practical guidance over legal language.
Businesses are willing to pay for compliance visibility.
Regulatory information can be structured into reusable knowledge models.
AI can improve comprehension without replacing professional advice.

12. Risks
Regulatory Accuracy
Risk:
Incorrect information damages trust.
Mitigation:
Human-reviewed knowledge base.

Regulatory Changes
Risk:
Requirements become outdated.
Mitigation:
Regulatory update monitoring.

AI Hallucinations
Risk:
Fabricated compliance information.
Mitigation:
Source-based retrieval and verification.

Local Government Complexity
Risk:
Requirements vary significantly by location.
Mitigation:
Progressive geographic expansion.
